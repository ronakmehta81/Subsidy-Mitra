Services

MSME Subsidy

Mapping your project to relevant central and state schemes such as capital subsidy schemes, interest subvention, Gramin Udyog Subsidy and other sector specific incentives. Designing the project cost and funding pattern in a way that maximizes subsidy eligibility, improves cash flows and reduces EMI burden over the loan tenure. End to end hand holding subsidy eligibility assessment, documentation, coordination with banks and departments, application filing, and post sanction compliance so the enterprise actually receives and retains the benefits.

Project Finance

Covers the full lifecycle of raising term loans and project finance, from planning to disbursement, using bank acceptable reports and models. Preparation of detailed project reports (DPRs), financial models and CMA data covering project viability, projected P&L, cash flow, DSCR, break even and sensitivity analysis in formats preferred by banks and NBFCs. Structuring the optimal mix of promoter contribution, term loan, working capital and subsidies to improve serviceability and increase chances of approval at competitive interest rates. Lender ready documentation support and liaison, organizing all business, financial and project documents, responding to bank queries, and coordinating through appraisal until sanction and disbursement, helping sustain a high approval ratio.

Virtual CFO Services

Virtual CFO support provides strategic financial leadership without the fixed cost of a full time CFO, helping management take data driven decisions. Budgeting, forecasting and MIS: monthly budgets, cash flow forecasts, profitability and variance analysis, and management dashboards so promoters see the business performance clearly and on time. Profitability and cash flow improvement: product/segment wise margin analysis, cost optimization, pricing support and working capital management to improve bottom line and liquidity. Strategic finance advisory: guidance on funding mix, banking relationships, covenants, compliance, and investor or lender communication so that growth is sustainable and financially disciplined.

Statutory Approvals, License & Certifications

This service ensures the business is properly registered and continuously compliant with applicable laws, licenses and sector approvals in India. Typical scope includes Identifying all mandatory registrations and licenses such as entity incorporation, GST, Udyam, Shops & Establishment, FSSAI, pollution and factory licenses, sector regulators (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, etc.), as applicable to the business model. Handling end to end filings, renewals and event based compliances (changes in directors, capital, address, etc.) and maintaining a compliance calendar to avoid penalties or interruptions in operations. Coordinating with consultants and authorities for inspections, certifications and periodic returns so daily operations remain smooth and legally compliant.
