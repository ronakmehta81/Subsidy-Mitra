Statutory Approvals

Wealth Design LLP helps MSMEs and startups in Hisar, Haryana obtain all key statutory approvals, licenses and certifications required for compliant, bankable and expansion-ready projects – from Udyam and ISO to CLU, HSPCB and Occupation Certificates.

Statutory Approvals, License & Certifications for SMEs

Wealth Design LLP provides end-to-end support for statutory approvals, licenses and certifications so that your MSME or startup can start, expand and operate without compliance hassles in Haryana and across India. From entity registrations to plant permissions and operating consents, the team handles documentation, liaison and follow-up with departments to save promoters' time and reduce approval risks.

The process begins with a compliance gap review of your business model, location and sector, followed by a clear roadmap of all approvals required before and after commissioning. You receive checklists, timelines and department-wise filing plans so that funding, construction, operations and subsidies are never delayed because of missing approvals.

Approval & Licensing Process

01 Compliance Mapping

Identify all licenses, NOCs and registrations needed based on industry, land use, pollution category, food safety, export/import and labour norms, with a phase-wise approval plan.

02 Documentation & Filing

Prepare application forms, project reports, building plans, process notes and compliance documents and submit them online/offline to concerned authorities in the correct formats.

03 Department Liaison

Coordinate with Town & Country Planning, Fire, PWD, Forest, HSPCB and other departments, respond to queries, arrange inspections and track file movement till approval or license grant.

04 Post-Approval Support

Assist with renewals, amendments, expansion approvals, change in land use, capacity enhancement and compliance for subsidies, bank funding and rating upgrades.

Key Registrations, Licenses & Certifications

  • Haryana Udyam Registration: Online MSME registration to unlock priority sector benefits, cheaper credit, subsidies and government scheme eligibility; complete guidance on documents and portal filing.
  • ISO Certification: Support for ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and other standards including gap analysis, documentation and audit readiness to improve quality, win tenders and boost customer trust.
  • ZED Certification: Assistance in MSME ZED registration, documentation and assessment to access subsidies on certification costs and improve "zero defect, zero effect" manufacturing practices.
  • Startup Registration (State & Centre): Advisory for DPIIT and state startup recognition, including eligibility assessment, pitch decks and documentation to access tax benefits, incentives and grants.
  • Import and Export Code (IEC): IEC registration and DGFT portal support so your business can legally import raw materials or export finished goods and claim export incentives.
  • FSSAI Registration & License: FSSAI basic, state or central license for food businesses with help on category selection, layout plans, hygiene processes and online filings.
  • CLU Approval (Change in Land Use): End-to-end support for Change of Land Use applications in Haryana including land records, layout, scrutiny replies and approvals from Town & Country Planning.
  • PWD Entry & Exit Permission (NOC): Permissions for approach roads, entry/exit on state or national highways connecting your factory, warehouse or commercial project to public roads.
  • Fire Department Permission (NOC): Fire safety plan preparation, equipment compliance and coordination with Fire Department for NOC issuance before building occupation.
  • Forest Department Permission (NOC): Clearances where land abuts protected areas or involves tree cutting, including applications, compensatory plantation and compliance conditions.
  • NOC from DTP & STP Haryana: Planning and sewerage-related approvals from District Town Planner and Sewerage/Service authorities for layout, connectivity and infrastructure compliance.
  • Building Plan Approval (DTP & STP): Architect coordination, drawings as per bye-laws and filing of building plans for industrial, commercial and warehousing projects for timely construction start.
  • Consent To Establish (CTE) – HSPCB: Pollution category assessment, emission/effluent details and HSPCB online applications to obtain CTE before installing plant and machinery.
  • Consent To Operate (CTO) – HSPCB: Assistance with post-commissioning compliance data, sampling reports and renewal applications to keep your industry legally operational.
  • Warehousing Development Registration & Approval: Support in getting approvals and registrations for warehouses and logistics parks so that they comply with planning, safety and storage norms.
  • Occupation Certificate (Completion): Guidance for obtaining completion/occupation certificates from Town Planning authorities after construction, ensuring your property is legally occupiable and financeable.
  • 1. Why are statutory approvals important for MSMEs?
    Proper approvals reduce closure risk, unlock subsidies, enable bank funding and ensure eligibility for government tenders and large corporate contracts.
  • 2. How long do approvals and licenses usually take?
    Timelines vary by department – some registrations like Udyam or IEC can be done in a few days, while CLU, CTE/CTO and building approvals may take several weeks depending on file scrutiny and inspections.
  • 3. Can you help for existing non-compliant units?
    Yes, support is available for regularisation, delayed registrations, renewal of expired licenses and migration to new schemes such as Udyam, ISO or ZED where applicable.
  • 4. Do I need all approvals before applying for bank finance?
    Banks normally check core approvals like Udyam, CLU, CTE/CTO, building plan and key licenses, so a clear approval roadmap improves sanction chances and speeds up loan processing.
