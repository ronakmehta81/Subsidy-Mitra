Cold Storage Project – Subsidy up to ₹10 Crore

Government Approved Scheme ke under Cold Storage & Integrated Cold Chain projects ko mil sakti hai capital subsidy up to ₹10 Cr 💰

Agar aap Fruits, Vegetables, Dairy, Meat, Frozen Food ya Agro Products ke liye Cold Storage start ya expand karna chahte hain, toh yeh scheme aapke liye strong opportunity ho sakti hai.

✅ Eligible Components:

✔ Machinery & Racking System
✔ Refrigeration Setup
✔ Insulated PUF Panels
✔ Pre-Cooling & Ripening Chambers
✔ Reefer Vehicles (as per scheme norms)

Proper DPR Preparation, Bank Loan Sanction aur Ministry Approval ke saath project approval smoothly ho sakta hai.
