Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Last Updated: June 18, 2026

Welcome to Wealth Design LLP ("we," "our," "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal and business data is handled in a safe, secure, and transparent manner. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you visit our website (https://wealthdesignllp.com) and utilize our services, including our integrations with third-party platforms such as Google and Meta APIs.

Wealth Design LLP is a premier financial consulting and project advisory firm specializing in Government Subsidies, MSME Consulting, Project Finance, Corporate Finance, Loan Facilitation, and statutory compliance. By using our website and services, you consent to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our consulting and financial advisory services:

  • Personal Identification Data: Name, email address, mobile number, mailing address, and business details when you fill out forms, request callbacks, or book a consultation.
  • Business & Financial Data: Information regarding your business structure, MSME status, project proposals, financial requirements, loan records, and other documents submitted for subsidy consulting and project financing.
  • Google Account Data (Google Login API): If you choose to log in or register on our platform using your Google Account, we receive your basic public profile information, including your name, email address, profile picture, and unique Google identifier. This is used solely to authenticate your identity and set up your account.
  • Meta Interaction Data (Meta APIs & Pixel): If you connect with us through Meta services (such as Facebook or Instagram login/interaction APIs), or if you browse our site where the Meta Pixel is integrated, we may receive basic profile data or usage analytics (such as pages visited, clicks, and advertising responses). This is used for marketing, ad optimization, and responding to your social inquiries.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, and website usage patterns collected automatically via cookies and tracking technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following business purposes:

  • To assess your eligibility and guide you through Government Subsidies (e.g., PMEGP, KVIC, Haryana Gramin Udyogik schemes).
  • To structure and process Project Finance, bank loans, and Virtual CFO services.
  • To authenticate and manage your user account via Google Login.
  • To contact you, provide updates, and respond to your callback requests or inquiries.
  • To optimize our marketing campaigns, measure ad performance, and target relevant business offerings using Meta APIs.
  • To maintain safety, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable statutory rules and legal obligations in India.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosures

We value your privacy and do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following scenarios:

  • Financial Institutions and Government Bodies: With your explicit consent, we share project details, CMA data, and financial documents with commercial banks, NBFCs, or government departments to secure subsidies and project financing.
  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in website hosting, authentication services (like Google), and marketing/analytics platforms (like Meta). These providers are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government authority to protect our legal rights, property, or safety.

4. Google & Meta Integration Compliance

Our applications utilize official Google and Meta APIs to enhance user experience. We strictly adhere to their respective developer policies:

  • Google API Services User Data Policy: Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies: We process any data obtained through Meta APIs in compliance with the Meta Platform Terms and Meta Developer Policies. We only request permissions necessary for our application's functionalities.

5. Meta & Google Data Deletion Request Instructions

If you have registered on our platform or interacted with us using Google Login or Meta APIs and wish to request the deletion of your personal data associated with these applications, you can do so easily:

To request deletion of your account and personal data:

  1. Send an email to ronakmehta09@zohomail.in with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your registered name, email address, and the service used (Google or Facebook/Instagram).
  3. Our support team will process your request, delete your profile data from our active databases, and notify you within 7-10 business days.

Additionally, you can revoke our access to your Google/Meta accounts directly via your account settings (e.g., Google Connected Apps settings or Facebook settings under "Apps and Websites").

6. Data Security

We employ administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse. However, please be aware that no transmission of data over the internet or wireless network is 100% secure.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (such as Meta Pixel and Google Analytics) to personalize content, analyze traffic, and improve website navigation. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, though some features of our site may not function properly as a result.

8. Your Legal Rights

Under Indian laws (including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to access, update, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Grievance Officer.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
